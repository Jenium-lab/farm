01 · About

Our ducks live outdoors, the way ducks are meant to.

Shree BajraBarahi Farm is a small family farm in Chapagaun, Lalitpur. Our ducks roam free over open pasture all day, forage on natural feed, and rest beside clean water. We collect their eggs each morning — nothing artificial, nothing rushed.

Open pasture

Ducks range freely on open ground, foraging through the day under the open sky.

Natural feeding

Simple, natural grain and pasture feed — no shortcuts, no artificial boosters.

Collected daily

Fresh eggs gathered every morning and handled gently, nest to delivery.
