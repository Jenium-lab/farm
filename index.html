01 About

Our ducks live outdoors, the way ducks are meant to.

Shree BajraBarahi Farm is a small family farm in Chapagaun, Lalitpur. Our ducks range freely over open pasture through the day, forage on natural feed, and rest beside clean water. We collect eggs every morning — nothing artificial, nothing rushed.

Outdoor pasture

Our ducks range freely on open ground, foraging through the day under the open sky.

Natural feeding

Simple, natural grain and pasture feed — no shortcuts, no artificial boosters.

Collected daily

Fresh eggs collected every morning and handled gently from nest to delivery.
